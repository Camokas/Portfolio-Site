## A whole world in small textures

I drew these assets from scratch in Photoshop during IsLands development, from June 2025 to August 2026. I used ChatGPT-generated reference images while creating the artwork myself. The collection includes nine atlases, with 787 named sprite regions taken directly from the Unity project. Some regions are animation frames or parts of a larger object.

The 16 × 16 items are my favourite part: despite their size, I wanted each one to be recognisable and to fit alongside the others. Keeping that readability at low resolution was the main challenge. I checked the artwork in-game dozens of times and adjusted it in context.

## From block faces to interface elements

The atlases cover separate block faces, character and armour sprites, grass, trees, NPCs, structures, items, and an early version of the game interface. That interface later became procedural and shader-driven. The viewer also includes artwork that did not make it into the game; the original Sprite Editor names and boundaries are preserved, including regions of different sizes.
