## A wooden character

I liked the toy-like, simple, inanimate appearance of a wooden doll. It also seemed a flexible base for future skins and cut-out details. I started with a high-poly model, simplified it, and completed the UVs; the final textures remained unfinished.

I made the model and rig myself. Some early AI-generated images served as references. This version of the character was used during development, but the corresponding game build has been lost.

## Building a mechanical rig

I built the rig from scratch while following YouTube lessons, including a robotic-arm rigging tutorial. The doll moved at its joints rather than bending like a soft human body. Working with IK was the most useful part of this process: the result was convenient to pose and animate.

The browser viewer shows the exported joint skeleton. The original Blender control rig is shown in the process screenshot.

## October 2024 · The detailed tree

I wanted more detailed, varied trees. The high-poly version was used in development, but its heavy geometry was cumbersome to work with. As optimisation became a priority, I moved towards a different visual style.

These archived screenshots show the detailed tree during texturing and in Unity scenes. They document an intermediate version; the trees did not make it into the final build while optimisation was still in progress.

## November 2024 · A simpler tree

I built a new low-poly tree instead of simplifying the old one. Its crown started as a deformed sphere, with the final foliage appearance handled by a shader. I focused on the amount of geometry visible to the camera and on reducing draw calls.

For the game, I made LOD levels manually with help from retopology and simplification plug-ins, without a billboard. The viewer here contains the supplied LOD0 export.
