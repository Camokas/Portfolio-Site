## A small world of props

I made the models in about a week in September 2026, during the August–September development of Misform. This page brings together 20 exported models from a larger set of roughly 25–30 weapons, interior objects, and props.

I modelled, unwrapped, and textured everything myself. I used Pinterest images and ChatGPT-generated images as references. The models and their textures were created manually.

## Desert Eagle

The Desert Eagle is one of the models I am most proud of. I was unsure I could make a weapon I would be happy with; the finished shape, topology, and UV layout exceeded my own expectations.

## Working at texture scale

The PS1 look comes from low-poly shapes, a muted greyish palette, and low-resolution textures without blur filtering. The challenge was to keep materials simple and readable after reducing the textures, rather than spend time on details that would disappear.

I worked in Blender and Substance Painter. Unity imported the .blend files directly, so saving an edited model updated it in the project. I exported low-resolution textures from Substance Painter, then assembled the materials and adjusted normal and metallic settings in Unity.
