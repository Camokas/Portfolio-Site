## A first deck with a history

I made this pack in August 2024 for an early deck in a Unity card-game prototype. I wanted it to look realistic and old. The card itself was a simple two-sided plane; most of the visual work went into the packaging.

I created the model and its artwork myself. The separate flaps were prepared to make opening the pack easier to animate in Unity.

<Models index={1} group open />

## Painting over the UV layout

I exported the UV layout from Blender to Photoshop and built the texture in layers: mixing textures, painting with brushes, arranging vintage-style decorative elements, and choosing and treating the lettering. UV work and this way of texturing were the main learning experience for me.

<Image index={1} group open />

## The game prototype

The idea involved collecting cards and using their abilities in a top-down 3D battle view. The project reached the menu and deck-opening animation, without progressing into a complete game. I also worked on procedural card animation in Unity; those movements are not included in this GLB export.
