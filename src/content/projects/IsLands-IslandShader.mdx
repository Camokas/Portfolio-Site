>  The first fully developed shader combining billboard functionality with dithered transparency. It automatically aligned sprites to face the camera and used proximity-based dithering to gradually fade objects near the player. This solved character occlusion issues and provided a shader-based alternative to script-driven billboard rotation, eliminating the need for per-object rotation scripts.
