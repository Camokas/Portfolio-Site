## Learning a second platform

I started by reposting YouTube Shorts, but they often attracted less than half the views and engagement. Around January 2026, I shifted towards videos made specifically for TikTok, alongside my YouTube work.

<StoryPanel kind="reach" group />

## What the results meant to me

My leading TikTok reached roughly twice the views of my leading YouTube Short in the captured statistics. I also observed more viewers joining Telegram from TikTok, although I did not measure referrals separately.
