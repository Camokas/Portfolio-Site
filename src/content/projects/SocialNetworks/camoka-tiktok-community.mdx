## Learning a second platform

I started by reposting YouTube Shorts, but they often attracted less than half the views and engagement. Around January 2026, I shifted towards videos made specifically for TikTok, alongside my YouTube work.

<StoryPanel kind="reach" group />

## What the results meant to me

My leading TikTok reached roughly twice the views of my leading YouTube Short in the captured statistics. The stronger response encouraged me to keep developing a separate format and brought more viewers into Telegram.

<StoryPanel kind="tiktok-results" group />

## The community changed the game

Player requests influenced development priorities, including multiplayer, which I might not have attempted without that encouragement.

Substantial AI assistance became part of IsLands development about a quarter of the way through its timeline.
