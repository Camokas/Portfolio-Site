## A development diary that became a community

I used the channel as a development diary, mainly about IsLands. Shorts introduced game systems and updates; longer videos shared original music and the Misform trailer.

Publishing daily or every other day taught me to turn small development steps into stories people could follow. Comments became a source of ideas for both the next video and the game.

<StoryPanel kind="youtube-channel" group />

## From Shorts to Telegram

Shorts brought viewers into Telegram, where a brief video could become a longer discussion or a playtest. That closer feedback loop helped turn viewers into participants in development.
