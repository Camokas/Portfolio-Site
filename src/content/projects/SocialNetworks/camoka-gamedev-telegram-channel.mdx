## Two chapters of one channel

The channel began as personal notes and later became a public IsLands development diary, with viewers coming from YouTube and TikTok.

## A place to share and listen

I prepared posts, answered questions, moderated discussions, collected bug reports, and shared test builds. Polls and shared play sessions helped involve players in development.

## Preparing a release

A release meant preparing the build, writing a readable changelog, making posts, and editing new videos. I generated cover images and then reworked them in Photoshop. New Year and Halloween events were occasional additions to this routine.

<StoryPanel kind="release" group />

## What feedback changed

Players encouraged me to attempt multiplayer. I used bug reports and polls to help choose the next tasks. Substantial AI assistance with game scripts became part of development roughly a quarter of the way through the project timeline.

<StoryPanel kind="feedback" group />

## Closing this chapter

I closed the channel after burning out and reassessing my relationship with publicity, media presence, and game development.
