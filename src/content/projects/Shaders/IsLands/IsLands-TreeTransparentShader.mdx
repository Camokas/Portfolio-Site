The block shader samples a texture atlas and changes the material with the weather. Rain increases metallic response and lowers roughness; snow uses the opposite adjustment and an unfinished pixel-noise snow covering.

The crouching indicator highlights the surface the player can walk on. It visualises the movement restriction; the shader does not enforce it.
