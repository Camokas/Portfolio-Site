## Where I started

CS:GO montages were an early way to learn editing through something I loved. I recorded gameplay, picked highlights from hours of footage, and cut them to music for myself and friends. I handled the recording and editing myself; a typical montage took about a day.

<Video index={1} group open />

## Timing, tracking, and masks

I focused on precise musical timing and making each kill feel impactful. One technique I explored was cutting out the kill feed from a screenshot, tracking it to the defeated opponent, and adding effects in After Effects.

<Video index={2} group open />

## A foundation for later work

I selected and cut footage mainly in Vegas Pro while learning Premiere Pro and After Effects. Tracking, masks, and built-in effects gave me a foundation I still use. A weak PC pushed me to optimise recording and work with low game settings.

As I played less, I moved on to editing other kinds of footage. These selected videos were published in February and April 2021, after the main period shown above.
