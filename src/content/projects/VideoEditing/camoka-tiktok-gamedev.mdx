## Editing for TikTok

I built each edit around a single game moment or joke. Filming the monitor with a phone gave the footage a different character from direct screen capture; the edit needed to make the idea clear immediately.

<StoryPanel kind="reach" group />

## One creator, from script to render

I handled the complete production. AI occasionally helped develop ideas and scripts; I recorded the narration and created the visuals myself.

<StoryPanel kind="solo" group />

## An hour to test an idea

Occasionally I could finish a TikTok in 30–40 minutes. A lighter production process let me test ideas more often and refine the next edit through practice.

<StoryPanel kind="time" group />

## Filming a monitor

I chose scenes that read clearly on a phone and filmed the monitor with simple lighting. Slightly defocusing the camera often reduced bands and flicker at the cost of fine detail. I then refined the cut, text, and colour for a quick, readable video.
