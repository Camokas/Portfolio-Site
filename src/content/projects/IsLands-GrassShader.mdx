>  A stylized smoke shader combining animated noise and Voronoi patterns to shape the smoke. Gradient mapping controls the color palette, while quantization creates a distinct stepped appearance. Billboard geometry automatically faces the camera.

> The shader was ultimately not used in the final project. Looking back, I can see opportunities to improve its visual quality and have a clear idea of how I would approach a redesign.
