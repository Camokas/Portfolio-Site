## Before the new day begins

I wanted Warm Horizon to feel like the first warm light of a new digital day: there is still time to stop, enjoy the sunrise, and listen. It became another track for IsLands.

I built up the arrangement with instruments, sounds, and background layers, looking for a character between Adventure and Lazy Algorithm. Keeping it varied while preserving that gentle atmosphere was the main challenge.
