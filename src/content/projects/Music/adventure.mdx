## From Buildest The Game to IsLands

Adventure began long before its platform release in July 2025. I wrote it for Buildest The Game, then kept returning to it as I gained experience. After that project closed, the track found a place in IsLands.

I gradually enriched the melody with synthesizers and other instruments, adjusting volume curves and sound parameters. I wanted a distinctly synthetic but welcoming sound for a calm, carefree world. The hardest part was finishing the long sequence of revisions with a result I liked.
