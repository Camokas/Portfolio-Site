## Under a virtual night sky

I imagined lying on the ground after a hot, tiring day, looking up at a virtual starry sky. Nixell was written for IsLands, with a darker, cooler mood and an emphasis on sustained sounds rather than percussion.

The arrangement develops through new instruments and changes to sounds already playing. My main challenge was keeping a calm track interesting to listen to without making it feel repetitive.
