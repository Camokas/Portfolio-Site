## Letting the plans wait

Lazy Algorithm is about a game day when there were important plans, but exploring the world and trying things out felt more appealing. I wrote it for IsLands with a carefree, deliberately lazy mood.

I used sustained electronic sounds with a brighter, livelier character than Nixell, gradually adding instruments and changing their parameters. The challenge was making that relaxed feeling enjoyable both during play and as a track on its own.
